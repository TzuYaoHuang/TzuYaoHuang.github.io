#### Hydromechanica (MT2461-25)
*TU Delft, Fall 2025*  
*Undergraduate fluid mechanics 101 for maritime student*

I help in TA sessions to help them know the physics behind some daily life example.
I make some exercises out of [CD Hovercraft](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A4PpVMKvu50), and [drag-minimizing ducklings](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-fluid-mechanics/article/waveriding-and-wavepassing-by-ducklings-in-formation-swimming/94759A0FF7070D9D7CAC5907594B1781?utm_campaign=shareaholic&utm_medium=copy_link&utm_source=bookmark).
I also run [some cool simulation](https://github.com/TzuYaoHuang/FallingAxisymmetricObject) to explain the effect of the shape on drag coefficients

#### Numerical Ship Hydromechanics (MT44025)
*TU Delft, Winter 2025, Winter 2025*  
*Graduate numerical course on panel methods and viscous flows applied in maritime context*

I gave a guest lecture on the state-of-the-art of the volume-of-fluid methods and give some demonstration on how to maintain proper mass conservation and interface sharpness. Here is the [demo on github](https://github.com/TzuYaoHuang/InterfaceAdvection.jl/tree/demo/VOF).
